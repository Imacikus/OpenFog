OpenFog is an open-source map exploration app. The world map starts hidden under a fog layer and is gradually revealed as you travel. Features GPS tracking, GPX/KML/KMZ import, achievements, and a leveling system.

Features:
- GPS tracking to reveal areas in real-time
- Import GPX, KML, or KMZ track files
- Level and XP system based on revealed area
- 8 achievements to unlock
- Full offline support via PWA service worker
- No ads, no tracking, no analytics
